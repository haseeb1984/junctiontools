About This Tool

Review the stages of an ecommerce checkout and identify common friction points that can reduce completed purchases. Use the analyzer as a structured checklist when reviewing forms, shipping, payment and trust elements.

How to Use It

Enter the requested funnel details and review each stage. Use the findings to prioritize changes, then compare conversion data before and after the change.

How It Works

The tool evaluates observable checkout factors and produces heuristic guidance. It does not have access to your private analytics unless the tool explicitly asks for data, so recommendations should be validated against real customer behavior.

Practical Example

If customers reach payment but frequently leave before completing an order, review payment errors, unexpected fees, account requirements, delivery information and mobile usability before changing the entire checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool measure actual conversion rate? Only when you provide the relevant data; otherwise it provides a structured diagnostic.

Can it identify the exact reason a customer leaves? No automated heuristic can determine intent with certainty.

Should recommendations be tested? Yes. Use analytics and controlled experiments where practical.
