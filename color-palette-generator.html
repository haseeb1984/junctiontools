About This Tool

Generate coordinated color combinations for websites, interfaces, branding concepts and visual projects. Use the resulting palette as a starting point for primary, secondary, accent and supporting colors.

How to Use It

Choose or generate a base color or palette, review the resulting swatches and copy the values needed for your design system.

How It Works

A palette generator can derive related colors through mathematical relationships such as hue shifts, complementary relationships or variations in lightness and saturation. Generated harmony is a design aid, not a guarantee of accessibility.

Practical Example

A brand team can start with one primary blue, generate supporting colors, then test the resulting text/background combinations with a contrast checker before using them in production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a generated palette guarantee accessibility? No. Check foreground/background pairs with an accessibility contrast test.

Can I use the colors in CSS? Yes. Copy the displayed color values into CSS variables or other design tokens.

Is the palette a final brand system? It is a starting point; brand decisions should also consider context, contrast, print and product guidelines.
