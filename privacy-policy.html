Privacy Policy

At Junction Tools (operated by Junction Systems), your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Data Processing & Server Interactions

While many utilities on Junction Tools process data directly in your browser using JavaScript, certain advanced tools utilize secure server-side processing to perform calculations and evaluations. We may use basic analytical tools to understand website traffic and improve user experience. No personal identifying data is collected or sold to third parties.

Our platform includes references to our parent network Junction Systems. We are not responsible for privacy practices on third-party websites.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please reach out through our Contact Page.
