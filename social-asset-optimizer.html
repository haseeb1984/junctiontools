Free Social Image Resizer & Asset Optimizer

100% Client-Side Processing. Crop, resize, and compress media assets locally for social platforms with zero data loss or privacy concerns.

How to Use Instructions:

  1. Click Upload Image to select any local image file from your device.
  2. Select a social media preset from Platform Preset or manually adjust the target Width and Height in pixels.
  3. Choose your output Format (JPEG, PNG, WEBP) and adjust the Quality slider as needed.
  4. Click Process & Resize Image to generate the live preview, then click Download Optimized Image to save it.
